# Phase 1 Scouting Brief — Heat / Wind / Drainage method families
*Tan Suyuan (topic-researcher). Scouting only — not a report. Input to the outline planner.*

## 1. Research summary

### 1.1 Urban heat / heatmap generation
Three families. **(a) Satellite LST.** Landsat 8/9 TIRS (100 m thermal, delivered at 30 m), MODIS MOD11 (1 km, daily) and ECOSTRESS (ISS, ~70 m, sub-daily, also yields ET) measure *land-surface* temperature — a skin temperature, not air temperature and not what a person feels. Retrieval is split-window or single-channel radiative transfer needing emissivity and atmospheric correction. Real kelvin, but snapshot, cloud-limited, coarse, and *cannot respond to a design edit*. **(b) Physically-based microclimate.** SOLWEIG (in UMEP) computes mean radiant temperature (Tmrt) and comfort indices (UTCI, PET) from a building-height raster plus meteorological forcing, via sky-view-factor, shadow and short/long-wave radiation modules — raster and open source, but needs a DSM/DEM and surface temperatures. ENVI-met is full 3D CFD microclimate (flow + plant + radiation) — the neighbourhood gold standard, hours per run. Both are design-responsive but far past numpy-in-seconds. **(c) Proxy indices.** LCZ (Stewart & Oke 2012, 17 classes at 100s-of-metres scale) and land-cover heat-exposure indices — cheap, deterministic, exactly what a raster sandbox can do, but relative, not degrees. **Honest limit:** a land-cover index may claim only "hotter/cooler than that surface, all else equal" — never a temperature. Cheap defensibility upgrade: add a solar/shadow (SVF) term from the building-height raster in numpy, and anchor the relative index to a published HK Landsat LST.

### 1.2 Wind / ventilation
The HK governing method is the Air Ventilation Assessment under **Technical Circular 1/06 + Annex A Technical Guide**. Indicator = Wind Velocity Ratio **VR = Vp/V∞** (pedestrian 2 m ÷ boundary-layer top ~400–600 m). Three stages: Expert Evaluation → Initial Study → Detailed Study. Wind tunnel is recommended for Initial and Detailed; CFD "may be used with caution", more admissible for Initial, and TC 1/06 states there is *"no internationally recognized guideline or standard for using CFD in outdoor urban scale studies."* Reports Site/Local spatial-average VR (SVR/LVR); higher is better; 16 directions; Detailed requires all 16 with wind-tunnel profiles, Initial may reduce directions if ≥75% frequency. Critically, TC 1/06 says the framework *"does not provide an absolute benchmark standard"* — pass/fail is comparative. Reference wind data: HKO Waglan Island. **Geometry-only screening** is Frontal Area Index/Density (FAI/FAD/FAR = λf; Grimmond & Oke lineage, reviewed 2022): frontal area ÷ plan area per direction; cheap, needs only footprints+heights; but a *shape* indicator, not flow — no velocity, turbulence or threshold. Corridor identification uses least-cost-path / cost-surface methods (2023–2026) and LCZ approaches. **Honest limit + the axis-aligned problem:** for an axis-aligned rectangle, projected width = a·|sinφ| + b·|cosφ|, so FAD is symmetric about N–S and E–W — NE, SE, SW, NW score identically. Since the published HSK prevailing wind is NE, a symmetric FAD cannot separate the direction that matters from its opposite. Fix: give each footprint a real orientation (or rasterise and compute true per-cell projected width) to break the symmetry. Even then FAD remains a proxy, never a VR.

### 1.3 Drainage / pluvial flood
SCS-CN (TR-55): Q=(P−Ia)²/(P−Ia+S), Ia=0.2S, S=25400/CN−254 (mm) — cheap and standard, but CN is a lumped parameter sensitive to soil group and antecedent moisture, and peer-reviewed critiques stress it is not physically based. HAND (height above nearest drainage) is a fast terrain-based inundation method with documented limits (no bathymetry, levee burning, catchment isolation at high stage, non-dynamic). D8 (O'Callaghan & Mark 1984) / D-infinity routing; Priority-Flood (Barnes et al. 2014) fills depressions in O(n)/O(n log n). Full 2D: LISFLOOD-FP (raster shallow-water solvers), CA-ffé (cellular automata, fast), TELEMAC. Blue-green/SuDS/sponge-city retention reduces runoff; InVEST Urban Flood Risk Mitigation does exactly CN-based per-pixel runoff reduction. **Honest limit:** the boundary is between a *screening ledger* (does this design change the volume of runoff that has nowhere to go?) and a *hydraulic model* (where, how deep, when). A level-pool allocation is neither routed hydrology nor a flood map. Cheap honest upgrades: D8 fill-and-spill routing + mass-balance assertion; HAND as a visual cross-check.

## 2. Source pool
1. [Housing, Planning and Lands Bureau / ETWB Technical Circular No. 1/06 — Air Ventilation Assessments (incl.
